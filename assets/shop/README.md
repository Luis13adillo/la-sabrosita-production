# `assets/shop/` — photographs of the actual shop

Everything else under `assets/` is product media on white. This folder is the
place itself: the storefront, the counter, the seating, the signage. It exists
because the website had no picture of the shop at all until 2026-08-26, and that
was the single biggest thing holding the design back — every image on the page
was a cut-out on a transparent background, which caps how much personality a page
about a real place can have.

## Naming

Same convention as `products/`: `LS_<Subject>_<KIND>_v01.<ext>`.

    LS_Storefront-Window_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg
    LS_Storefront-Banner_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg
    LS_Counter_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg
    LS_Seating-Booth_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg
    LS_Neon-Swedesboro_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg

`ORIGINAL` is the untouched file as it came off the phone. If one is rotated,
straightened or colour-corrected, that is a NEW file at `_v02`, never an
overwrite — same rule as everywhere else in this repo.

## What must not happen here

**No synthesised or composited storefront.** Two of the photographs show the shop
as it is today; a Street View capture also exists showing the building BEFORE the
renovation, under previous signage. Producing an image that shows the shop looking
like something it does not is a picture of a real business that is not true, and a
customer who drives to Kings Highway on the strength of it has been misled. Use
the real photographs. Lay them out, crop them, grade them — do not invent one.

The pre-renovation Street View image is not in this folder on purpose.

## What each one is for on the site

| Photograph | Where it earns its place |
|---|---|
| Counter with the mark | The story band — replaces four product cut-outs floating on purple, which are doing no work |
| Seating booth + neon | The visit section — it is the evidence for "seating inside and outside" |
| Storefront window | Find-us — the decal is literally what a customer scans the street for |
| Sidewalk banner | Find-us, secondary |
| I ♥ SWEDESBORO neon | Its own moment. See below. |

## The I ♥ SWEDESBORO neon

This is the most valuable image here and it should not be spent as decoration.
Neither michoacana.com nor heladosmexico.com can say anything like it — they are
national packaged brands, and belonging to one town is the thing a shop has that
they do not. It is the argument for the shop being *of* Swedesboro rather than
merely located there.
