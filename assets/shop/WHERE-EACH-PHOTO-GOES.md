# Where each shop photograph goes

Five photographs were sent on 2026-08-26. Four are usable; one is not. When the
files land in this folder, this is the plan.

## 1. The counter, with the mark on it, lit pink
`LS_Counter_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg`

**Goes in the story band**, replacing four product cut-outs currently floating on
purple doing no work. That band is supposed to say who this shop is, and right now
it says it with the same cut-outs that appear forty-five more times further down.
A photograph of the actual counter ends that.

## 2. The booth, with the I ♥ SWEDESBORO neon above it
`LS_Seating-Booth_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg` · `LS_Neon-Swedesboro_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg`

**Two jobs.** Cropped to the seating it is the evidence for the visit section's
"seating inside and outside" — a claim becomes a fact you can look at. Cropped to
the neon it replaces the drawn sign in the town band with the real one.

## 3 & 4. The storefront window and the sidewalk banner
`LS_Storefront-Window_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg` · `LS_Storefront-Banner_ORIGINAL_v01.jpg`

**A find-us moment beside the address.** The decal on the glass is literally what
a customer scans the street for, and the banner reads ¡NUEVA TIENDA! which is
worth showing while it is still true. Both arrived rotated 90 degrees; they get
straightened into `_v02` files rather than overwriting the originals.

## 5. The Google Street View capture — NOT USED
This shows the building BEFORE the renovation, under the previous tenant's
signage. It is not in this folder and should not be added.

The client asked whether the images could be combined "like if La Sabrosita was
already established". Laying the real photographs out together is ordinary design
work and is what the plan above does. Synthesising an exterior that shows the shop
looking like something it does not is a different thing, and the answer is no: a
customer who drives to Kings Highway on the strength of a picture that was never
true has been misled, and it is the shop that wears that. The real storefront
photographs are good, and they are what get used.
